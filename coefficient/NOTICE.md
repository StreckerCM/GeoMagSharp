# Coefficient File Attribution

## Bundled Models

The following geomagnetic model coefficient files are included in this package:

### WMM2025.COF - World Magnetic Model 2025
- **Source:** NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information (NCEI)
- **Download:** https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/world-magnetic-model
- **License:** U.S. Government work, public domain (17 U.S.C. 403)
- **Valid:** 2025.0 - 2030.0

### WMMHR.COF - World Magnetic Model High Resolution 2025
- **Source:** NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information (NCEI)
- **Download:** https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/world-magnetic-model
- **License:** U.S. Government work, public domain (17 U.S.C. 403)
- **Valid:** 2025.0 - 2030.0

### WMM2015.COF - World Magnetic Model 2015
- **Source:** NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information (NCEI)
- **License:** U.S. Government work, public domain (17 U.S.C. 403)
- **Valid:** 2015.0 - 2020.0 (historical reference)

### IGRF12.COF - International Geomagnetic Reference Field, 12th Generation
- **Source:** International Association of Geomagnetism and Aeronomy (IAGA)
- **Download:** https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/IAGA/vmod/igrf.html
- **License:** Public domain
- **Valid:** 1900.0 - 2020.0

## Models NOT Included

### EMM - Enhanced Magnetic Model
- **Source:** NOAA NCEI
- **Download:** https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/enhanced-magnetic-model
- **Note:** Requires completing a survey form before download. Users must obtain this file directly from NOAA.

### BGGM - BGS Global Geomagnetic Model
- **Source:** British Geological Survey (BGS)
- **Website:** https://geomag.bgs.ac.uk/research/modelling/BGGM.html
- **Note:** Commercial license required. Contact BGS for licensing.
